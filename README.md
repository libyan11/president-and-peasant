# President & Peasant

## ▶ [Play the game](https://libyan11.github.io/president-and-peasant/)

https://libyan11.github.io/president-and-peasant/

The President card game in your browser, on phone or computer. Play online with friends, or against the computer.

**Play with friends:** one person taps *Create a room* and shares the 4-letter code or link. Everyone else types the code and taps *Join room*. Up to 6 players, and the host can fill empty seats with computer players. If someone drops out, the computer plays their cards until they come back.

**Rules**
- 3 is low, 2 is high. Every 6 and both jokers are wild.
- The 3♦ starts the first game. After that, the President starts.
- Whoever starts a round picks single, pair or triple (never more than 3 cards). Everyone matches that number of cards, same number or higher, or passes. The number of cards stays the same until the round ends.
- You can't finish on a wild card: your last play can't include a 6 or a joker. If you end up holding only wild cards, you're stuck, get skipped, and lose.
- The 3♠ played alone kills the round.
- Once four cards of one number are down in a round (wilds count), the table clears and whoever put the fourth card down starts.
- When everyone else passes, it goes back to whoever put the last cards down.
- The game ends when only one player still has cards. Then titles are given out and the Peasant and President swap cards.
